FAQ

How do I lock?

Go to Streamflow's token-lock, connect your wallet, pick $MOONBAG, choose an amount and an unlock date at least 24 hours out. That's it — our engine reads your lock straight off the chain within seconds. Locks shorter than 24 hours don't qualify for rewards.

Is it safe? Who holds my tokens?

Not us — and that's the point. Your tokens sit in Streamflow's audited on-chain escrow, controlled by the lock contract you created, and unlock automatically on the date you chose. Nobody — not the dev, not this site — can touch them. We only read the locks and send SOL out.

When do I start earning?

23 hours after your lock is created. Fresh locks show as "warming up" on the leaderboard until then. Why the wait? It makes lock-right-before-the-drop sniping impossible — you have to actually hold. Once warmed up, your lock earns on every drop until it expires.

Which wallet gets paid?

The lock's recipient wallet — for a normal Streamflow self-lock, that's the wallet you locked with. SOL lands there every drop, no claiming needed.

Where do the rewards come from?

pump.fun pays token creators a fee on trading volume. Those creator rewards are auto-claimed every minute and 100% redistributed to lockers. No team cut is taken from the pot beyond a tiny SOL reserve for transaction fees.

What if my payout is tiny?

Dust payouts aren't lost — anything below the payout minimum rolls over and accumulates until it's worth sending. You'll get it.

Can I unlock early?

Your lock unlocks exactly when your Streamflow contract says it does — that was your choice at lock time. When it expires, withdraw on Streamflow; expired locks stop earning immediately.

Is this financial advice?

No. This is a memecoin. Rewards depend entirely on trading volume and may be zero. Locked tokens can't be sold until your lock expires — that's the entire point, and also the risk. Never lock more than you can afford to have stuck (or go to zero) for the duration.

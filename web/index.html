FAQ

How do I lock?

Send $MOONBAG from your wallet to the vault address (shown above once live). The wallet you send from is the wallet that gets paid. Locking more later just adds to your position.

Where do the rewards come from?

pump.fun pays token creators a fee on trading volume. 100% of those creator rewards are claimed by the vault and redistributed to lockers. No team cut is taken from the pot beyond a tiny SOL reserve for transaction fees.

What if my payout is tiny?

Dust payouts aren't lost — anything below the payout minimum rolls over and accumulates until it's worth sending. You'll get it.

Can I unlock?

Unlocks are supported by the vault — but every unlock reduces your share of every future drop the moment it happens. That's the point.

Is this financial advice?

No. This is a memecoin. Locked tokens are transferred to a vault wallet — that requires trusting the vault operator. Assume anything you put in could go to zero. Never lock more than you can afford to lose entirely.
